# Multicity manifests

`ACTIVE_STAGE.json` is the single human-readable runtime state for ongoing
cross-city work.

`PLAN_READINESS.json` and the versioned review records are historical
provenance. Completed city records remain under `cities/`, and cross-city
terminals remain under `reviews/portable_predictor_contract/`.

Do not delete partial completed checkpoints when resuming the active stage.
